About Yorkshire Terrier Guide

Yorkshire Terrier World is edited by Laura Bennett for owners of a tiny terrier with a fearless attitude. Dental disease, weight charts, tracheal collapse and grooming are the main topics.

Laura is not a veterinarian. Health articles are based on veterinary references and published research, linked inline, and they flag clearly when a Yorkie needs a vet.

Yorkies are small, but their care questions are not. The site tries to answer them properly.

Editorial Policy

Guides on Yorkshire Terrier Guide are written by Laura Bennett with the help of AI tools. Every statistic, cost figure and health claim has to come from a source we have opened and read, and that source is linked next to the claim. If something can't be confirmed, it doesn't go in.

We are not veterinarians and never present ourselves as such. Health articles explain what the research says and when to see a vet; diagnosis and treatment belong with your own veterinarian.

Articles are reviewed when new data appears or a reader reports an error through the contact page.
